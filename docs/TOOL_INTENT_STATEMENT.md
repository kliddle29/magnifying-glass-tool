# Tool Intent Statement

*Left blank on purpose — this is the module's reflective deliverable, and
it needs to be a record of what you believe about behavior, not a guess
an assistant made on your behalf. Fill in each section before the
Usability Evaluation stage.*

## 1. Core Function

What does the tool actually do?

> It magnifies whatever's under your cursor. That's the whole function.

## 2. Human Context

Describe the real situation this tool enters. What pattern or loop does
it address?

> Small text is everywhere on a computer, and a browser zoom button
> doesn't help once you're outside the browser. This tool doesn't care
> what app you're in. Bigger, right where you're looking.

## 3. Intended User

Imagine a specific person.

> Someone with eyesight issues. Honestly, someone like me. I wear
> glasses/contacts, and squinting at small text on a screen isn't a
> hypothetical problem for me, it's a daily one.

## 4. Behavioral Thesis

What does this tool believe about behavior change?

> Understanding comes from getting close enough to actually see
> something, not from being told what it is. Google Lens already does
> the identify-and-explain thing, and does it well. I'm not trying to
> build a worse version of that. Bigger. Clearer. Closer. That's the
> whole job.

## 5. Dignity Clause

How does this tool avoid shame, surveillance, or manipulation?

> It doesn't send anything anywhere. Nothing you point it at gets saved,
> logged, or sent to a server; it never even touches the network. There's
> no score, no streak, no history of what you looked at. It also doesn't
> run unless you turn it on, so it isn't watching in the background
> either.

## 6. Refusal Clause

What will this tool intentionally NOT become?

> An AI that tells you what you're looking at. I actually built that
> version first: it took whatever the lens was showing and had Claude
> describe it. Then I ripped it out. Not because it was broken. The
> second the tool starts interpreting the picture for you, it stops
> being a magnifying glass and turns into something else wearing one as
> a costume. This one just makes things bigger. Nothing more.

## 7. Atmospheric Tone

What should it feel like to use?

> Minimal. Like a plain, honest magnifying glass that happens to work
> on your whole screen instead of a piece of paper. Not an app with
> opinions about what you're doing.
